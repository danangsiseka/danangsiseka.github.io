About

A work in progress.

This website is a long-term record of what I am learning, creating, researching, and exploring.

I am Rasya Danang Kurnia Siseka, publicly known as Danang Siseka, a high school student at SMAN 1 Sidoarjo.

My academic interests include physics, chemistry, mathematics, biology, engineering, and science and technology. I also enjoy programming, writing, literature, music, chess, sports, and other areas that give me opportunities to learn something new.

I see curiosity as an important part of development. This site brings together work that would otherwise be scattered across different platforms and serves as an archive that can grow with me.
